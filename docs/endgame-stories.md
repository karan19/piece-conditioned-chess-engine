# Endgame Stories

The fourth homepage feature lives at `/endgame-stories/`. It is a separate Vite entry alongside Guided Play, Opening Studio, and Board Recall. It reuses the chessboard and existing endgame opponent for practice.

## Teaching experience

One hundred and eighty-one curated studies currently have independently checked variations, including: Majestic Trap, Pawns of Destiny, Most Majestic Game, The Knight’s Return, Majestic Victory, Tricky Deal, Majestic Bishops, Majestic Pawn Chase, Knight Cube, The Forbidden Win, Sublime Draw, Majestic Glory, Esacriats, Majestic Misplacement, KNIGHTMARE, Knight+Rook VS Queen, Majestic Defense, Majestic Shield, Out of The Woods, Sacrifices of Glory, Ultimate Bishop, Majestic Style, Majestic Geometry, Revolutionary, The Great Trap, Two Pawns Against a Rook, Majestic Precision, and Majestic Victory: The Rook Promotion. Most offer multiple plans; each has a board that advances only on request, commentary for both sides after each move, a checked endpoint result, reflection questions, and a practice game from the starting position. A narrowly scoped defensive study can have one guided line when its complete draw proof also checks the declined-sacrifice alternative. Guided lines include drawing errors and a tablebase correction to the source's early central-route explanation. They are selected instructive variations, not a proof tree covering every legal move.

Examples:

- `/endgame-stories/KEnJIrhWSDQ/?line=keep_shield&ply=1`
- `/endgame-stories/NysivKCHQiY/?line=recover_center&ply=3`
- `/endgame-stories/KEnJIrhWSDQ/?mode=practice`

Practice uses the existing public tablebase and Stockfish fallback. It saves under `pcce-story-practice-<video-id>` and does not affect existing opening or endgame sessions.

## Source collection

The imported archive contains all 513 channel entries, including 248 endgame-related candidates selected from title and narration keywords. This is a discovery filter, not an assertion that every candidate is an endgame or every endgame has been recognized. The full channel remains searchable with `scope=all`.

429 videos have captions. Available source walkthroughs pair full state-duration caption spans with 18,125 compressed board snapshots. These are sampled video images, not FENs or verified moves. Source walkthroughs retain attribution and link to the exact video moment. Videos without usable images offer commentary and original-video access. The known wrong crop Z7SQg2fLB70 is excluded from imagery. Other snapshots must not be silently promoted to verified chess data.

Records are loaded on demand from `public/endgame-stories/<id>.json`; images load only when displayed. The small catalog and structured studies are bundled separately from other feature entries. Source images are reduced to a maximum of 384 pixels and encoded as WebP, preserving the external extraction originals.

## Rebuild and validation

With Pillow installed:

```sh
python3 scripts/build-endgame-stories.py /path/to/pietro-checkmate-shorts
npm test --workspace @pcce/web
npm run build --workspace @pcce/web
```

The importer reads v3 TSV with `quoting=csv.QUOTE_NONE`, preserves all video IDs, checks exclusion metadata, and records source manifest hashes in `src/stories/data/provenance.json`. Curated FENs and outcomes come from the previously checked lesson artifacts. Tests replay all 568 branches, check commentary coverage, confirm critical teaching contrasts, legal en passant and the promotion-mate finish, and verify every source image reference.

Narration and video imagery belong to their original owners and are attributed to Pietro Checkmate. The repository's MIT license covers project code, not third-party source content.
